Bio

I'm an incoming PhD student at the Institute of Data Science, University of Hong Kong (HKU), supervised by Prof. Yanchao Yang and Prof Yi Ma. Before that I also got my bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from HKU with a First Class Honour.

My research interests lie in 3D Vision, 4D Human/Object Understanding. I am particularly interested in developing methods for abstracting high-quality multimodal and multidimensional representations from real-world data, and in leveraging such representations to advance embodied AI and world models. Currently, I'm working on projects related to Human-Scene/Human-Object Interaction.
